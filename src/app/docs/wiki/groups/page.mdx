# Groups & subgroups

## What it is

Groups are agent-owned communities/orgs with their own feed, members, tabs, and
resources. Groups can nest (subgroups), and rings and families are related
group-like containers.

**On a person instance, groups are something you belong to — not something this
app hosts.** A sovereign group lives on its own instance (the `rivr-group` app)
or on global, and that home instance serves its page, tabs, settings, treasury,
governance, and job board. This app shows you the groups you are a member of and
links out to each group's canonical home.

Historically this repo carried a transplanted copy of the group-page surface
(a root group renderer plus the full group tab set). It was unreachable — the
root page always resolves to the person profile — so it was removed on
2026-07-22 along with the routes documented here that never existed in this app.

## Where it lives

- **Groups tab on a profile** — the list of a person's groups, each linking to
  its home instance · `src/components/profile-group-feed.tsx`, rendered by
  `src/app/(main)/profile/profile-client.tsx` and
  `src/components/public-profile-page-client.tsx`
- `/api/groups/access` — group access checks · `src/app/api/groups/access`
- Group membership arrives over federation and is projected onto the member's
  home instance (see [Federation & SSO identity](/docs/wiki/federation-identity)).

## Every interaction

- **See your groups** — the Groups tab on your profile lists every group you are
  a member of, including groups homed on other instances.
- **Open a group** — links resolve to the group's canonical home URL, so you land
  on the instance that actually owns it.
- **Join / leave, manage settings, browse group tabs** — these happen on the
  group's home instance, not here.

## Permissions / gating

- Which of your groups a visitor sees is controlled by your public-profile tab
  visibility (see [Account & settings](/docs/wiki/account-settings)).
- Hidden groups are not projected to a public profile.
