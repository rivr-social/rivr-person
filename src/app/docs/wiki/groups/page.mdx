# Groups

## What it is

**On a person instance, groups are something you belong to — not something this
app hosts.**

A group lives on its own instance (the `rivr-group` app) or on global, and that
home instance serves its page, tabs, settings, treasury, governance and job
board. What this app does is show you the groups you're a member of, and send you
to each one's real home.

## How to see and open your groups

1. Go to `/profile` and open the **Groups** tab.
2. Every group you belong to is listed — including groups homed on other
   instances entirely.
3. Click one. The link resolves to that group's **canonical home URL**, so you
   land on the instance that actually owns it, not on a broken local route.

Your groups also appear on the home feed's **Groups** tab (`/?tab=groups`).

Joining, leaving, managing settings and browsing a group's tabs all happen on the
group's home instance.

## Good to know

- **Membership arrives over federation.** When you join a group elsewhere, that
  membership is projected back onto your home instance, which is why the tab can
  show groups this app has never hosted.
- **Visitors see your groups only if you let them.** The Groups tab is one of the
  tabs governed by your per-tab profile visibility — see
  [Account & settings](/docs/wiki/account-settings).
- **Remote links open as real cross-origin links**, not in-app navigation. That's
  deliberate: prefetching another origin's page is what used to cause a flash of
  error on load.

<Callout type="warning" title="This app has no group routes">
  There is no `/groups`, `/groups/[id]`, or any group tab surface here — no
  directory for groups, rings, families or projects exists in this codebase at
  all. This repo once carried a transplanted copy of the group-page surface; it
  was unreachable, because the root always resolves to the person's profile, and
  it was removed on 2026-07-22 along with the routes previously documented on
  this page.
</Callout>

## Under the hood

- Groups tab · `src/components/profile-group-feed.tsx`, rendered by
  `src/app/(main)/profile/profile-client.tsx` and
  `src/components/public-profile-page-client.tsx`
- Link resolution · `src/lib/federation/entity-link.ts`
  (`resolveEntityHref` / `resolveRemoteHomeBaseUrl`) rendered through
  `src/components/canonical-link.tsx`
- `/api/groups/access` — the REST side of password-protected group membership:
  challenge, check, revoke, renew
