# Account & settings

## What it is

`/settings` is where you configure yourself and your instance: who you are,
what's public, how you get email, your seller account, your custom domain, and
your recovery key.

Every control on this surface is wired to something real. In a deliberate
truth-in-UI pass, preferences this app cannot honour were **removed rather than
left looking functional** — the write-only privacy matrix, the text-size and
colour-theme pickers, and the push / event-reminder / new-message toggles are
all gone. What's left does what it says.

## The tabs

Nine tabs, in this order:

| Tab | What's in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Account** | Identity, photos, locale, skills, personal-profile fields |
| **Privacy** | Per-tab public-profile visibility — the privacy control this app enforces |
| **Notifications** | Email on/off, Murmurations publishing |
| **Appearance** | Dark mode |
| **Assistant** | Your agent: identity, roles, knowledge, voice, access, connectors |
| **Connectors** | A signpost to the Assistant tab |
| **Seller** | Stripe Connect status and balances |
| **Federation** | Custom domain, peer status, PeerMesh and Bluesky links |
| **Security** | Your recovery seed |

Deep-link any of them with `?tab=<value>`. Above the tabs sits an **App Version**
card showing the current and latest release, the channel, and buttons to update
the instance or read the changelog.

<Callout type="note" title="Personas take over this page">
  If a persona is your active actor, `/settings` renders that persona's editor
  instead of these tabs — the whole tab strip is replaced, not trimmed. Switch
  back to your controller identity to reach the settings below.
</Callout>

## How to edit your profile

Go to **Account**. You get:

- **Change Photo** (avatar), plus **Profile Photos** — up to 8 images that
  appear in your profile's Photos tab even when they're not attached to a post.
- **Name**, **Username**, **Email**, **Phone**, **Tagline**, **Bio**.
- **Home Locale** — a searchable select plus a map/chapter picker.
- **Social Links** and **Skills** (type, press Enter, click × to remove).
- A **Persona / Personal Info** block: Gene Keys, Human Design, Western and
  Vedic astrology, Big Five, Myers-Briggs, Enneagram.

Press **Save Changes**. Updates are sparse — only the fields you supplied change.

## How to control who sees what

This is the part worth reading carefully, because privacy here works at **two
levels**:

1. **Per item.** When you create a post, an offering, or a document, you choose
   its visibility there. That is the authoritative content boundary, enforced
   server-side when anyone loads your profile.
2. **Per tab.** The **Privacy** tab decides which *tabs* appear on your public
   profile at all.

To set tab visibility:

1. Go to **Privacy** → **Public Profile Tabs**.
2. For each of **About, Posts, Docs, Media, Events, Groups, Photos, Offerings,
   Activity**, choose one of:
   - **Public** — visible to everyone (the default)
   - **Connections** — visible to your connections only
   - **Only Me** — visible to you only
   - **Hidden** — the tab is completely hidden
3. Press **Save Tab Visibility**. This saves separately from the main form.

> 📸 **Screenshot:** _The Privacy tab's per-tab visibility editor, with a row per profile tab and the Public / Connections / Only Me / Hidden select open._ — capture at `/settings?tab=privacy`. _(placeholder — remove when image added)_

<Callout type="warning" title="Your own view is not a preview">
  On your *own* public profile you see all nine tabs regardless of these
  settings — including ones you marked Hidden. To check what a stranger sees,
  view your profile signed out. Note also that tab visibility hides the **tab**;
  it is the per-item visibility that actually withholds content.
</Callout>

## How to set up selling

<Callout type="warning" title="First-time setup belongs on the profile Wallet tab">
  Start at `/profile?tab=wallet` → the **Become a Seller** card → **Set Up
  Payout Account**. That path asks **"Where is your bank?"** before creating
  anything, and a Stripe account's country **cannot be changed afterwards**.
  The Settings → Seller button below does *not* ask, so it creates a
  United-States account regardless of where you actually bank. Use Settings for
  checking status and finishing a half-done onboarding, not for the first step.
  See [Marketplace](/docs/wiki/marketplace#how-to-sell).
</Callout>

The **Seller** tab's card is titled **Stripe USD Wallet**.

Press **Set Up Stripe USD Wallet** (or **Complete Setup** if you left onboarding
half-done), finish Stripe's hosted flow, and come back. You'll then see three
status rows — **Details submitted**, **Charges enabled**, **Payouts enabled** —
a badge reading **Active** or **Limited**, your **Available** and **Pending**
Stripe balances, and a link into the Stripe dashboard.

Full detail in [Marketplace](/docs/wiki/marketplace).

## How to put this instance on your own domain

Go to **Federation** → **Custom Domain**.

1. Enter your domain and press **Set Domain**.
2. The page lists the **Required DNS Records**. Add them at your registrar.
3. Press **Verify DNS** and read the verification results.
4. Once active, you can **Generate Traefik Config** and copy it for your proxy.

> 📸 **Screenshot:** _The Federation tab showing the Custom Domain card with required DNS records and verification results._ — capture at `/settings?tab=federation`. _(placeholder — remove when image added)_

The same tab shows your federation node state: **Node Active** or **Node Not
Enabled**, your base URL, and counters for **Trusted Peers**, **Queued Events**,
and **Exported Events**. Below that you can link a **PeerMesh Spatial** identity
(paste an export JSON or a manifest URL) and a **Bluesky / AT Protocol** handle
(handle plus an app password, which is used once and never stored).

<Callout type="note" title="This is a different kind of domain from the builder's">
  The Federation tab points a domain at **the whole instance**. The
  [Builder](/docs/wiki/builder) separately binds domains to **published sites**.
  Two different features, two different settings.
</Callout>

## How to look after your recovery key

Go to **Security**. This tab shows your **Recovery Seed** — a fingerprint, when
it was created or last rotated, and two actions.

**To reveal your seed phrase:**

1. Press **Reveal seed phrase**.
2. Request a code by **email** or **SMS**, then enter it.
3. Enter your **device passphrase** to decrypt, and copy the phrase.

**To rotate it:**

1. Press **Rotate recovery seed** and pass the same challenge.
2. Set and confirm a **new device passphrase**.
3. Write down the new phrase, then press **Complete rotation**.

> 📸 **Screenshot:** _The Security tab's Recovery Seed card showing the fingerprint badge and the Reveal / Rotate buttons._ — capture at `/settings?tab=security`. _(placeholder — remove when image added)_

<Callout type="warning" title="The Security tab can be empty — that's correct">
  The panel renders nothing at all unless this instance runs in sovereign mode.
  Hosted-federated deployments never issue seeds, so there is nothing to show.
  SMS delivery for the challenge is currently stubbed; use email.
</Callout>

Your seed is generated **in your browser**. The server only ever stores a public
key and a fingerprint — it never sees the mnemonic or the private key.

## Notifications and appearance

**Notifications** has exactly two switches and a save button:

- **Email Notifications** — turning this off suppresses every non-essential
  email this instance sends you.
- **Publish to Murmurations** — publishes your eligible public profile data to
  the Murmurations network index.

**Appearance** has exactly one control: **Dark Mode**.

## Good to know

- **Connections are managed in one place.** The **Connectors** tab is a signpost
  that switches you to the Assistant tab, where the real connector panel lives.
- **Two owner pages aren't in any menu**: `/settings/visitor-access` and
  `/settings/outgoing-email`. See [Instance owner](/docs/wiki/admin).
- **Notifications** at `/notifications` is a separate page, with tabs **All,
  Unread, Following, Reactions, Comments**, per-row read toggles and a
  mark-all-read button that acts on the current filter.

<Callout type="warning" title="Two things this page used to promise and shouldn't">
  There is **no Billing tab** — the settings tab list has nine entries and none
  of them is billing. And there is **no data-export feature anywhere in this
  app**: no "export your data" button, no account-archive download. Membership
  billing is handled through the `/products/membership-*` pages and Stripe; see
  [Wallet & money](/docs/wiki/wallet-treasury).
</Callout>

  `/auth/signup/recovery`. Seven older per-field step routes are bare redirect
  stubs back to `/auth/signup` — `name`, `email`, `phone`, `birthday`,
  `login-method`, `save-login`, `confirm`. The eighth,
  `/auth/signup/terms`, is **still a real page**: it renders the Terms, Privacy
  Policy and EULA for acceptance and, on agree, routes to
  `/auth/signup/login-method` (which then redirects to `/auth/signup`)
