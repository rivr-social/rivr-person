# Locales & regions

## What it is

Place-scoped commons. Locales/chapters and regions/bioregions (basins) are
place-typed agents that posts, events, and offerings can be scoped to, giving
each place its own feed and directory. Rings and families are related
place/affinity containers.

## Where it lives

- `/locales`, `/locales/[id]` — locale commons · `src/app/(main)/locales/**`
- `/basins` — bioregions · `src/app/(main)/basins/**`
- `/rings`, `/families` — related containers · `src/app/(main)/rings/**`, `src/app/families/**`

## Every interaction

- **Browse places** — list locales, basins, rings, families.
- **Open a place** — its scoped feed of posts/events/offerings.
- **Scope content to a place** — when composing, attach a locale/region so it
  appears in that place's feed (resolve names → ids via `rivr.places.list`).
- **Participate** — join/participate per the place's membership model. _TODO(verify): confirm live locale/region join + role affordances._

## Permissions / gating

- **Public** place pages (`/locales`, `/basins`, `/rings`, `/families` are public
  prefixes).
- Participation and place-admin actions require a session and the relevant role.
- Sovereign locale/region instances federate to global for aggregation.
