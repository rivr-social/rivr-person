# Places

Locales, basins, rings, and families are **global and group concepts**. A person
instance federates *to* them; it doesn't host them.

What you can do here is **scope your own content to a place**. When you compose
a post or an event you can attach a locale or region, and that scoping travels
with the content when it federates outward, so it appears in that place's feed
on the instance that owns it. Your **Home Locale** is set in `/settings` →
**Account**, with a searchable picker and a chapter selector.

<Callout type="warning" title="These routes do not exist here">
  `/locales`, `/basins`, `/rings`, and `/families` have no pages in this app.
  Links to place-typed entities resolve to their canonical home — the sovereign
  instance that owns them, or the global aggregator — rather than to a local
  route.
</Callout>
