# Feeds & posts

## What it is

The social layer: a feed you browse, posts you write, and reactions and comments
on both.

On a person instance the **root of the site is your public profile**, not a
feed — visiting `/` shows people who you are. There is no separate multi-tab
feed underneath it: the profile *is* the browse surface.

## How to browse

`/` renders your public profile. It carries up to nine tabs — **About**
(default) · **Posts** · **Docs** · **Media** · **Events** · **Groups** ·
**Photos** · **Offerings** · **Activity** — and you switch between them by
clicking. The **Posts** tab is where the post feed lives for a visitor.

<Callout type="warning" title="The ?tab= parameter does nothing on the site root">
  The root page ignores query parameters entirely — the tab is client-side
  state that always starts on **About**. Two redirects in the app still point
  at it anyway: `/marketplace` sends you to `/?tab=marketplace` and `/events`
  to `/?tab=events`, and both land you on your profile's About tab. The search
  box has the same problem: submitting a query pushes `/?tab=posts&q=…`, and
  the query is dropped. The six-tab **Posts · Events · Groups · People · Gigs ·
  Mart** feed those URLs were written for is the *global* app's home page; the
  component ships in this repo but a person deploy never renders it.
</Callout>

Signed in as the owner, **`/profile` is the surface that does read `?tab=`** —
`/profile?tab=wallet`, `/profile?tab=offerings`, `/profile?tab=calendar` and so
on all work, across its thirteen tabs.

Everything else is reached by direct link, by the **bottom nav** (Home ·
Assistant · Create · Profile), or by the **command palette** — press `/` or
`Cmd/Ctrl+K`, or tap the ⁄ button above the nav bar. Job detail pages at
`/jobs/[id]` have no index and no link at all: you need the URL.

Open any post at `/posts/[id]`. The feed's comment button deep-links straight to
the comments with `?focus=comments`.

## How to write a post

1. Open the composer — `/create` (the **Post** tab) or the inline composer in
   the feed.
2. Choose the post type:
   - **Social** — an ordinary post
   - **Offer** — you're offering something
   - **Request** — you need something
   - **Gratitude** — thanking someone (you pick who)
3. Write. **Paste a URL and it becomes a rich link preview automatically.**
4. Optionally add one image.
5. Post.

**Offer and Request** posts can either link an existing offering or build one
inline — the **Configure Embedded Offering** dialog creates the offering when
you publish (its submit button reads *Use in Post*).

**Social** posts get one extra switch: **Mark as Live Invitation**, which shares
your location for **one hour** so people nearby can find you.

The composer's other tabs are **Event**, **Project**, **Group**, and
**Offering**; `/create?tab=job` is an alias that opens the Project composer.

## How to react and comment

**Reactions** — tap the reaction button to toggle a 👍 Like. Hover on desktop or
long-press on touch to open the full set: **Like, Love, Haha, Wow, Sad, Angry**.
Reactions work on posts *and* on comments.

**Comments** — write in the comment box and submit (Cmd/Ctrl+Enter works).
Comments are **threaded**: each one has a **Reply** affordance.

Both appear inline in the feed and on the post detail page.

## The design studio

There's a full canvas editor at **`/create/design`** — *Design Studio*, for
graphics, social cards, and flyers. Its **My Photos** panel pulls in your own
uploaded images.

<Callout type="note" title="Reachable by URL only">
  Nothing in the app links to `/create/design` today — no nav item, no command.
  You have to type the address. `/calendar` and `/badges` are different: they
  have no nav item either, but both are entries in the command palette's
  **Navigate** group, so `/` or `Cmd/Ctrl+K` will get you there.
</Callout>

## Good to know

- **Guests can read** public posts and post detail; composing needs a session.
- **Your feed is permission-filtered.** An anonymous viewer sees only publicly
  crawlable content, and that filtering happens *before* the page limit is
  applied — so private posts never eat an anonymous visitor's page budget.
- **Federated posts bounce home.** Opening a mirrored copy of a post redirects
  you to the instance that owns it.
- **Link previews are guarded.** Internal RIVR links resolve from the local
  database with no outbound request at all; external ones are fetched with an
  SSRF guard that refuses private and loopback addresses, plus a timeout, a
  body-size cap, and a rate limit.
- Posts created here project to global as signed `post.created` events. The
  composer's federation toggle is driven by `GET /api/federation/node-status`
  and tells you how many trusted peers the post would reach.

## Under the hood

- `/` · `src/app/(main)/page.tsx` → `home-or-instance.tsx`. On a person
  instance (`INSTANCE_TYPE=person` + `PRIMARY_AGENT_ID`) it returns
  `renderPersonPage()` → `public-profile-page-client.tsx`; `home-client.tsx`
  (the six-tab feed) is only reached by the non-person branch and is dead code
  on every deploy of this repo
- `/profile` · `src/app/(main)/profile/profile-client.tsx` — the one page that
  reads `?tab=`
- Nav + palette · `src/app/(main)/layout.tsx` mounts `bottom-nav.tsx` and
  `CommandBar.tsx`; both exist only inside the `(main)` route group
- `/posts/[id]` · `src/app/posts/[id]/page.tsx`
- `/create` · `src/app/(main)/create/page.tsx`; tab contract in
  `src/lib/create-tabs.ts`
- `/create/design` · `src/app/(main)/create/design/page.tsx`;
  media from `GET /api/design/media`
- Components · `create-post.tsx`, `post-feed.tsx`, `post-detail-client.tsx`,
  `reaction-button.tsx`, `comment-feed.tsx`, `link-preview-card.tsx`
- `POST /api/link-preview` · `GET /api/live-invitations` ·
  `POST /api/upload` (10 files per request, 20 uploads per hour)
- Post types · `PostType` in `src/lib/types.ts` — `social`, `offer`, `request`,
  `gratitude`
