# Wallet & money

## What it is

Your money lives on **one surface**: the **Wallet** tab of your own profile at
`/profile`. There is no separate `/wallet` page on a person instance.

That one tab holds four different kinds of value, which is worth untangling
before you use it:

- **Rivr USD wallet** — an in-app balance you top up by card and spend at
  checkout or send to another agent.
- **Stripe USD wallet** — money you have *earned* as a seller, sitting in your
  Stripe Connect account (shown with its pending portion). Only appears once
  you have a Connect account.
- **Thanks tokens** — the gift/mutual-aid side. Non-currency, and they **decay**
  if you sit on them (demurrage).
- **Crypto wallet** — an ETH address on file, used for USDC checkout.

Separately, **linked bank accounts** (via Teller) let you move real money in and
out, and **membership tiers** are bought as ordinary Stripe products.

## How to use your wallet

1. Go to `/profile` and open the **Wallet** tab.
2. The top row shows your balances: **Rivr USD Wallet**, **Stripe USD Wallet**
   (with pending), and your **Wallets** count. If you have a Connect account, a
   full-width **Total USD Balance** card sits above them summing the two.
3. Use the action row:
   - **Add Money** — opens the deposit dialog. It creates a Stripe
     PaymentIntent (`POST /api/wallet/deposit`) and credits your Rivr wallet.
     Minimum **$1.00**, maximum **$1,000.00** per deposit.
   - **Send Money** — transfer to another agent (transfers cap at **$500**).
   - **Transaction History** — the full history drawer.
   - **All Purchases** — jumps to `/profile/purchases`.

4. Below the balances are the **Thanks** cards — *Thanks Tokens* (current
   count), *Burned For Inactivity* (total removed by demurrage), and *Thanks
   Flow Ratio* (sent ÷ received, shown as ∞ when you've received none).
5. Then the **Bank Accounts** card and the **Crypto Wallet** card.
6. At the bottom, three sub-views:

| Sub-view | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Transactions** | Every wallet movement — description, date, type, status, amount |
| **Purchases** | Receipt cards for what you bought, plus a **Tickets** list badged *Wallet* or *Card* |
| **Sales** | Your Stripe Connect balance and payout controls, then your sale rows |

Each sub-view is deep-linkable: `?tab=wallet&walletTab=purchases`.

  `POST /api/wallet/deposit` enforces `RATE_LIMITS.WALLET_DEPOSIT` keyed on

  deposit bounds before creating the intent.
</Callout>

## How to connect a bank account

The bank lane is powered by **Teller** (not Stripe Financial Connections), and
it only lights up if this instance has Teller credentials configured.

1. On the Wallet tab, find the **Bank Accounts** card — *"Link bank accounts
   through Teller Connect, view balances here, and send supported payments from
   eligible accounts."*
2. Press **Connect Bank** (or **Connect Another Bank**). The app fetches a
   Teller Connect session nonce (`GET /api/wallet/banks/connect`, good for 10
   minutes) and launches Teller's own hosted widget.
3. Pick your institution and authenticate there. On success the widget hands
   back an enrollment, which the app posts to `POST /api/wallet/banks/enroll` —
   the server **verifies Teller's enrollment signature** before saving it. You
   get a **Bank connected** toast.
4. Your linked accounts then list on the card (`GET /api/wallet/banks`). The
   card also shows which Teller environment this instance is pointed at.
5. To move money out, initiate a payment (`POST /api/wallet/banks/payments`)
   with a source account, amount, and payee address — all three are required.
   Some payments trigger a Teller MFA step, which re-launches the widget.

> 📸 **Screenshot:** _The Bank Accounts card on the Wallet tab, showing a linked institution row._ — capture at `/profile` (Wallet tab, scroll to **Bank Accounts**). _(placeholder — remove when image added)_

<Callout type="warning" title="Not configured? The card stays empty.">
  If Teller isn't configured on this instance, `/api/wallet/banks` returns an
  empty account list and `/api/wallet/banks/connect` returns **503** with
  "Teller Connect is not configured on this instance." Nothing breaks — the
  card just has nothing to show.
</Callout>

## How to connect a crypto wallet

On the **Crypto Wallet** card you can either press **Connect MetaMask** (which
reads the address from the extension) or type an address into the ETH address
form. Once an address is on file, USDC checkout becomes selectable on
crypto-priced listings — see [Marketplace](/docs/wiki/marketplace).

## Memberships

Membership tiers are ordinary Stripe products with their own pages. Each page
offers **monthly** or **yearly** billing, and every displayed price already
includes a Connect surcharge of **$2/month** or **$24/year**
(`MEMBERSHIP_CONNECT_SURCHARGE_CENTS`).

These are the figures the pages actually show:

| Tier | Page | Monthly | Yearly (billed) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Basic | `/products/membership-basic` | $9 | $82 |
| Seller | `/products/membership-seller` | $24 | $207 |
| Host | `/products/membership-host` | $24 | $222 |
| Organizer | `/products/membership-organizer` | $46 | $391 |
| Steward | `/products/membership-steward` | $41 | $413 |

To subscribe: open the tier's page, toggle **monthly / yearly**, and check out.
The webhook (`POST /api/stripe/webhook`) grants the membership on settlement —
and mints a Thanks grant with it.

> 📸 **Screenshot:** _The Seller membership product page with the monthly/yearly toggle and the surcharge-inclusive price block._ — capture at `/products/membership-seller`. _(placeholder — remove when image added)_

<Callout type="warning" title="Treat the on-page 'Save $N' figures with suspicion">
  Each product page carries a hardcoded savings literal that is **not**
  recomputed against the surcharge, so it does not match the surcharge-inclusive
  prices above. The per-tier feature bullets are likewise marketing copy in the
  page files, not a rendering of any entitlement table — a bullet appearing on a
  tier is not proof that capability is gated to it.
</Callout>

## How to send thanks, money, or a voucher

Thanks tokens are the gift side of the economy — a way to recognise someone
without invoicing them.

1. Open the **thank** control where it appears alongside a person or a piece of
   their content.
2. Pick what you're sending: **thanks**, **money**, or a **voucher**.
3. For thanks, choose how many of your tokens to send and add an optional
   message. You get a **Thanks sent** confirmation.

Tokens you hold **decay if you sit on them**. A scheduled job
(`/api/cron/thanks-demurrage`) burns tokens for inactivity, which is what the
*Burned For Inactivity* and *Thanks Flow Ratio* cards are telling you: the
system is nudging you to circulate them rather than hoard them. Subscribing to
a membership also mints a Thanks grant.

## Good to know

- **`/profile/purchases` is narrower than it sounds.** It shows a **Purchase
  History** card containing **ticket purchases only** (`Amount · Fee · Total`,
  the payment method, and a *View event* link), plus an **All Wallet
  Transactions** card of your last 30 movements. Your marketplace receipts are
  *not* here — they're in the Wallet tab's **Purchases** sub-view.
- **The Stripe USD wallet only appears with a Connect account.** Set one up from
  `/settings` → **Seller**.
- **A "Release Test Sales To Wallet" button exists but only in Stripe test
  mode** — it hard-refuses unless the secret key starts with `sk_test_`.
- Whether visitors can see your Wallet tab at all is governed by your
  per-tab profile visibility — see [Account & settings](/docs/wiki/account-settings).

<Callout type="warning" title="What this instance does NOT have">
  A person instance is one person. There is **no** group treasury, no subgroup
  financial accounts, no issued spending cards, and no Stripe Treasury/Issuing
  surface here — those are group-instance capabilities. There is also no
  `/wallet`, `/points`, or `/subscribe` route: the Wallet tab, the Thanks cards,
  and the `/products/membership-*` pages are the real surfaces. **Points are not
  a currency on this instance** — the number beside your name on your profile
  header is a plain read-only field with no ledger, history, or spend surface.

</Callout>

- `GET /api/wallet/banks/connect` — Teller Connect session nonce

- `POST /api/wallet/banks/payments` — initiate a Teller payment
- `POST /api/wallet/banks/webhook` — Teller callbacks
- `POST /api/stripe/webhook` — membership grants and settlement
- `GET /api/cron/thanks-demurrage` — token decay
