# Messages

## What it is

Direct messages and group rooms, backed by Matrix. It's a real chat client
running in your browser — your instance holds Matrix credentials for you and the
page connects, syncs, and renders conversations live.

Everything happens on **one page**. There are no per-conversation routes;
selecting a conversation swaps the panel beside the list.

## How to use it

1. Go to **`/messages`**. You'll see *"Connecting to Matrix…"* while the client
   loads and performs its first sync.
2. The left side (a third of the width on desktop) is your conversation list
   with unread counts. The right side is the chat panel. On mobile the two swap
   places as you navigate.
3. Pick a conversation and type. Until you do, the panel reads *"Select a
   conversation to start messaging."*

### Starting a conversation with someone

Use the query parameter: **`/messages?user=<agentId>`**. The page resolves that
person's Matrix identity and opens — or creates — your DM room with them.

This is how the rest of the app links into messaging. The **Contact seller**
button on a purchase confirmation, for example, is just a link to
`/messages?user=<sellerId>`.

### Group rooms

A 1:1 DM can be **promoted into a group room** by adding participants; the app
creates the new room and moves you into it. Group rooms render through a
different panel from DMs and carry their own chat mode.

Leaving a room is how you delete a conversation.

## Good to know

- **The Matrix SDK is loaded lazily** — it's about 831 KB, so it's deliberately
  kept out of the app's initial JavaScript. That's the reason for the connecting
  screen.
- **Syncing stops when you leave the page**, and starts again when you return.
- **Sync repair is automatic**, with a bounded number of attempts; if it gives
  up, the page tells you rather than failing silently.
- Messaging needs a session and a provisioned Matrix identity.
- Matrix discovery is advertised at `/.well-known/matrix/client` and
  `/.well-known/matrix/server`, both derived from the instance's domain.
- If rooms are purged server-side, the owner can tidy up stale local records
  with `POST /api/admin/matrix/reconcile` — see
  [Instance owner](/docs/wiki/admin).

<Callout type="warning" title="There are no message sub-routes">
  `/messages/[otherUserId]` and `/messages/room/[roomId]` do **not** exist in
  this app — an earlier version of this page claimed both. The single
  `/messages` page plus the `?user=` parameter is the whole surface.
</Callout>

## Under the hood

- `/messages` · `src/app/(main)/messages/page.tsx` (lazy boundary,
  `ssr: false`) → `messages-page-client.tsx`
- Panels: `MatrixChatPanel` (DMs), `GroupChatPanel` (rooms)
- Server actions · `src/app/actions/matrix` — `getMatrixCredentials`,
  `getDmRoomForUser`, `getDmRoomForListing`, `getMatrixUserIdsForAgents`,
  `getUserGroupRooms`
- Configuration · `MATRIX_HOMESERVER_URL`, `MATRIX_SERVER_NAME`,
  `MATRIX_ADMIN_TOKEN`, `MATRIX_AGENT_IDS`
