# Badges

## What it is

Badges are credentials that recognise a skill or a qualification, and they gate
things — most visibly, whether you can claim a particular job. This app shows
you the badge catalogue, your progress through each badge's training, and which
badges you hold.

## How to use it

1. Go to **`/badges`** — *Community Badges*, "Earn badges by completing training
   and demonstrating skills."
2. The top of the page summarises where you stand: **Badges Earned**,
   **Available Badges**, and your completion rate.
3. Filter with the tabs — **All Badges**, **Earned**, **Available**. Each badge
   card shows its training duration and how many jobs it unlocks.
4. Press **Start Training** on a badge you don't hold, or **View Details** on one
   you do. Either opens `/badges/[id]`.
5. On the badge page you get a progress bar over the badge's modules and the
   training content itself, module by module. Earned badges show a green
   **Earned** marker.

![The badges page with its stat cards — Badges Earned, Available Badges, Completion Rate, Advanced Badges — and the All / Earned / Available tab strip over the badge grid](/wiki/badges-stake/person-26-badges.png)

Some badges offer a **live class**, which pre-fills the event composer so you can
schedule one.

## Good to know

<Callout type="warning" title="Training progress here is not real yet">
  Completing a module writes to your **browser's local storage only**. Nothing
  is persisted on the server, and **no badge is ever awarded by this UI** — you
  will see "Training completed!" and remain unbadged. Badges you actually hold
  are read from data written elsewhere (typically a group or the global
  instance, over federation). Clear your browser storage and your training
  progress is gone.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="There is no badge administration here">
  This app has no issue-badge, revoke-badge, or badge-admin surface of any kind,
  and no `/admin/badges` route. A previous version of this page claimed admins
  could issue and revoke badges from this instance. They cannot.
</Callout>

- **`/badges` has no nav item**, but it is in the command palette's **Navigate**
  group — press `/` or `Cmd/Ctrl+K` and pick *Badges*. Typing the URL works too.
- Badge pages are public, and they resolve a *unified* session, so a visitor who
  arrived via cross-instance sign-on still sees their own earned state rather
  than being treated as anonymous.
- Holding a badge is what unlocks claim and manage affordances on `/jobs/[id]`.

## About stake and points

**Neither is a user surface on this instance.**

There is no `/points` route and no points balance page — the number beside your
name on your profile header is a plain read-only field with no ledger, no
history, and nothing to spend. Stake exists in the codebase only as
group-scoped ledger aggregation with no page rendering it here; stake and
share-weighting are group and global concepts.

For the value surfaces that *are* real here — your wallet, thanks tokens,
memberships — see [Wallet & money](/docs/wiki/wallet-treasury).
